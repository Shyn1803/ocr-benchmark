It follows from [ Ber90 , Proposition 2.4.4 ] that for every irreducible component Y of U s , there is a unique point ξ Y ∈ U reducing to the generic point of Y . In the formalism of real valued forms and currents on Berkovich spaces [ CD12 ] , it is well known that d ′ d ′′ [ u ] | U is the discrete measure on U supported on the points ξ Y of U for Y an irreducible component of U s , with multiplicity at ξ Y equal to deg L ( Y ) . Wanner showed that subharmonicity of u on U is equivalent to the positivity of the current d ′ d ′′ [ u ] : see [ Wan19 , Theorems 1 and 2 ] and use the fact that X is locally algebraic [ vdP80 ] . Thus L s is nef if and only if u is subharmonic on U . Suppose that u is subharmonic at x . Shrinking U , we may assume that u is subharmonic on U . This implies that L s is nef, so it follows from [ GJR21 , Proposition 7.1 ] that L u ( x ) is nef. Conversely, if L u ( x ) is nef, then by [ GJR21 , Proposition 7.1 ] , we may shrink U so that L s is nef, which as above implies that u is subharmonic on U .
