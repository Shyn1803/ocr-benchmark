# Course Description:

This course in public rhetoric and practical communication examines the use of digital media as a demonstration of expertise, and gives students opportunities to create their own expertiserelated materials. Challenging the notion of expertise as a body of knowledge, we will consider expertise as a type of performance. This performance may be inscribed into a recorded form, such as a blog text or a YouTube video. It may also be performed for a live audience, as in a presentation. We will examine expertise demonstrations, how media affect the performance of expertise, and how individuals have created media-specific identities. Students will reflect on their own expertise, and learn how to efficiently demonstrate their authority through digital media. Students who have completed the 124 Practicum course are highly encouraged to focus on their practicum experience.
