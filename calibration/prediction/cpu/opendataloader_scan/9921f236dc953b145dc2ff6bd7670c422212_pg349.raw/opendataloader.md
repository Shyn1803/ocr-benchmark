# Success Factor Category: Environment

# Success Factor: Stable internal environment (strategic direction)  

A stable internal environment in terms of strategic direction of the outsourcing bank influences ITO success. Firstly, environmental changes can obviate desire for ITO in general. A change in strategic direction against ITO can be caused by a new internal management too. Secondly, environmental stability stable IT requirements for the lifetime of the ITO contract and thus avoids costly contractual amendments if possible at all.
