# Cross border co-ordination

Optimum timing of withdrawal likely to vary

Gains from information exchange and coordination:

Minimise cross-border distortions and arbitrage

Minimise collective action problems

Largest gains where spillover risks highest (such as deposit insurance and liability guarantees – eg Hong Kong/Singapore/Malaysia co-ordinating removal of exceptional retail deposit insurance)

Strengthening infrastructure and framework for foreign currency liquidity (G20 group)
