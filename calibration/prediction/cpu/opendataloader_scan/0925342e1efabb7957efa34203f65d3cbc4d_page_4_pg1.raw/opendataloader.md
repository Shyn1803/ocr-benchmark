By procuring and using environmentally preferable products and services, state governments have the perfect opportunity to substantially reduce the environmental impact of their own buildings and operations. Perhaps more importantly, in so doing states also demonstrate environmental leadership, increase public awareness of the beneﬁts of buying green products, and encourage economic development of greener goods by fostering markets for environmentally preferable products.

Increasingly, state governments have identiﬁed green cleaning as a key strategy in achieving their sustainability objectives. As a result, 22 states have now adopted a green cleaning procurement policy of some kind. Historically, these state green cleaning laws and policies have focused on state agencies or state-owned buildings.
