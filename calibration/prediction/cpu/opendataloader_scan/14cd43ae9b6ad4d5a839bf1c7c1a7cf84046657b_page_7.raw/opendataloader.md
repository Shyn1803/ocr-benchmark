# IV. DISCUSSION

The physics of hydrogen absorption in metals is strongly related to heterogenous catalysis on surfaces as in both cases, the crucial step of the involved processes is the chemisorption and dissociation of the gas(es) on the metal surface. 5,42 Therefore many ideas and results obtained from catalysis re- search can be used to explain hydrogen absorption. In particular; the switching kinetics of a switchable mirror may be seen primarily as a measure for the catalytic activity of its surface.
