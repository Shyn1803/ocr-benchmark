# SITING CONSIDERATIONS

• Understand fate of infiltrated water

- • Intent is to infiltrate to native underlying soil
- • Arterial ROW with dense underground infrastructure (preferential pathway → utility trenches)
- • Potential for excessive shallow interflow emerging at slopes, development cuts, or in basements
- • Use engineering controls
- • Ex. trench water stops to prevent re-infiltration to pipes
- • Ex. liners to protect adjacent infrastructure
