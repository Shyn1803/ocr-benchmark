# Types of Service

Services Day

Non-residential services to people with mental retardation including adult developmentaltraining, vocational rehabilitation, and community employment

Residential Services (24 hour staff)

Services necessary to maintain a person in their home or in some other residential setting by providing staffing support 24 hours a day.
