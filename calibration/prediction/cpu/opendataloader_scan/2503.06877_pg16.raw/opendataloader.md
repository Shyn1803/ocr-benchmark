# 6. CoNCLUSIONS

In this paper, we study the non-linear least squares (NLS) problem associated with a smooth variety M and a polynomial map ψ : M → R n . This model encompasses a wide range of practical problems, including low rank (structured) matrix approximation problems and low rank (structured) tensor approximation problems. Under mild assumptions, we prove that critical points of the NLS problem are all mapped into the smooth locus of ψ (M). One signiﬁcant implication of this result is that, when designing and analyzing algorithms for the NLS problem, misbehaved points are guaranteed to be avoided. As a consequence, we obtain a linear convergence rate of an iterative sequence generated by algorithms for the NLS problem. In particular, we apply our general framework to establish the generic linear convergence rate of the iAPD-ALS algorithm for the low rank partially orthogonal tensor approximation problem, without any further assumption. Given that numerous problems can be recast as the NLS problem for diﬀerent choices of M and

ψ , our framework is potentially applicable to the convergence analysis of numerical algorithms for them. It is possible to establish an unconditional convergence result analogous to Theorem 5.13. We also want to mention that our framework heavily relies on the geometry of M and ψ . Hence, the framework is applicable if the geometry of (M ,ψ ) is well-understood. Of particular interest is the case where ψ (M) is a variety of structured matrices or tensors, since such varieties are already extensively studied in the context of computational algebraic geometry [13,31,35].
