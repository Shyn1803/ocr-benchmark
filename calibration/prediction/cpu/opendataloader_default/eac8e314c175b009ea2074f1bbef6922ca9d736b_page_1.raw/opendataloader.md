Joining the list of popular culinary establishments in the downtown district is Ike’s Diner, located at 9476 Harding Avenue. The Diner was created by a partnership between long-time restaurateur Samir Elnomany and Kosher Chef Yossi Horowitz. The diner serves breakfast all day, pastas, salads, pizzas, ﬁ sh, sandwiches, ice cream by the scoop and delicious iced coﬀ ees. Ike’s Diner is serving from 7:30 a.m. – 11 p.m. every day except Saturday. Home deliveries are available. For more information, call (305) 397-8559.

Isaac Bashevis Singer, a 1978 winner of the Nobel Prize for literature and a leading ﬁ gure in Yiddish literature, was a resident of the Town in the 1980s. Now the Surfside street named in his honor has new, more prominent signage. A brief ceremony attended by residents and Town o ﬃ cials marked the unveiling of the signs on 95th Street. In addition to the Nobel prize, Singer also won two National Book Awards for his novels and short story collections. He died on July 24, 1991.

The Town Commission has approved renovations and new landscaping for two major parking lots in the downtown Surfside district. The ﬁ rst is the Abbott lot between 95th and 96th Streets (west of Harding Avenue behind the CVS and Flanigan’s). The other is the 94th Street lot (on the southeast corner of 94th and Harding Avenue). Contractors Lynx Construction and Luke’s Landscaping will be working with Public Works to complete the renovation. The project is scheduled to begin before the end of August.

Business Improvement District in Planning

On July 17th a public meeting was held to discuss the possibility of creating a Business Improvement District (BID) for Downtown Surfside. Redevelopment Management Associates (RMA) presented information on how BIDs work and described possible BID programs for Downtown Surfside. Similar to a Homeowners Association, but for commercial properties, a BID formalizes a process for property and business owners to work together to improve a commercial district.

### Streetscape Improvement Takes Next Step

The plans for the Harding Avenue Streetscape are now being reviewed by the Florida Department of Transportation, since Harding Avenue is a state-owned street. The Town is planning to start the project as soon as it is approved by the Florida DOT.
