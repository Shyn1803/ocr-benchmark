If G(x,y,S) ≡ 0 and ε = 0, then r(t) ≡ ̺0 and ϕ(t) ≡ ν(̺0)t + φ0 with arbitrary constants ̺0 and φ0. Numerical analysis of the system with G(x,y,S)  ≡ 0 and ε = 0 shows that, depending on the values of the perturbation parameters, two cases are possible. In the ﬁrst case, the amplitude of the solutions either tends to zero or grows until it reaches the boundary of the considered domain, depending on the sign of Q0 (see Fig. 1, a). In the second case, the solutions with an asymptotically constant amplitude r(t) ≈ r0 may appear (see Fig. 1, b). The emergence of such solutions is associated with the capture of the system into resonance. If ε = 0, the stochastic perturbations may disrupt such a behaviour (see Fig. 1, c).
