Remark 4.1. Each member of ADE-singularities is Newton non-degenerate.

Embedded toric resolutions of ADE-singularities were studied in [18, 19]. Here differently we construct regular refinements of Gr¨bner fans of each members of them. This leads us their embedded toric resolutions [1, 2]. Additionally, these resolutions will be minimal which means that:

Definition 4.2. An embedded toric resolution is called minimal if

- i) The vectors in the regular refinement are irreducible.
- ii) The resolution graph obtaining from the refinement does not have any −1


curve means the self-intersection of each vertex is different from −1 (this gives us the resolution graph is minimal).

Note that the self-intersection of a vertex is computed as: The number −s ∈ Z≤0 with s.v = mi=1 vi is called the self-intersection of the vertex v in the graph where each vi is adjacent to v in the regular refinement.
