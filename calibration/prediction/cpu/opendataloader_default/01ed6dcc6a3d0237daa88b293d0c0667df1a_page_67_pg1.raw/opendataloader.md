Violet Ratiometric Membrane Asymmetry Probe/Dead Cell Apoptosis Kit

 e Violet Ratiometric Membrane Asymmetry Probe/Dead Cell Apoptosis Kit (A35137) provides a simple and fast method for detecting apoptosis with dead-cell discrimination by  ow cytometry.  e violet ratiometric membrane asymmetry probe, F2N12S (4 -N,Ndiethylamino-6-(N-dodecyl-N-methyl-N-(3-sulfopropyl))ammoniomethyl-3-hydroxy avone, Figure 15.5.11), is a novel violet diode–excitable dye for the detection of membrane phospholipid asymmetry changes during apoptosis.  is dye exhibits an excited-state intramolecular proton transfer (ESIPT) reaction, resulting in a dual  uorescence with two emission bands corresponding to 530 nm and 585 nm and producing a two-color ratiometric response to variations in surface charge.59  is ratiometric probe is therefore a self-calibrating indicator of apoptotic transformation, which is independent of probe concentration, cell size and instrument variation, such as  uctuations of laser intensity or sensitivity of the detectors.

Given that apoptosis modi es the surface charge of the outer lea et of the plasma membrane, F2N12S can be used to monitor changes in membrane asymmetry that occur during apoptosis through a change in the relative intensity of the two emission bands of the dye59 (Figure 15.5.12).  e F2N12S-based apoptosis assay allows samples to be analyzed a er a 5-minute incubation at room temperature and does not require special bu ers or wash steps.  is kit can be paired with other reagents such as MitoProbe™ DiIC1(5) or annexin V for multiparametric analysis of apoptosis and viability.  e kit components, number of assays and assay principles are summarized in Table 15.4.
