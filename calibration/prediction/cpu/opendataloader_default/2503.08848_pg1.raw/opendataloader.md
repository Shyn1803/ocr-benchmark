Uniform convergence of Pfaﬃan point process to the Airy line ensemble

Zhengye Zhou

Abstract

We consider a family of Pfaﬃan Schur processes whose ﬁrst coordinate marginal relates to the half– space geometric last passage percolation. We show that the line ensembles corresponding to the Pfaﬃan Schur processes with geometric weights converge uniformly over compact sets to the Airy line ensemble. By detailed asymptotic analysis of the kernels, we can verify the conditions for the ﬁnite dimensional weak convergence introduced in [7]. By utilizing the tightness criteria of the line ensembles established in [8], we can further improve the ﬁnite dimensional convergence to the uniform convergence over compact sets. Moreover, using the same methodology we also show that sequences of spiked Pfaﬃan Schur processes converge uniformly over compact sets to the Airy wanderer line ensembles constructed in [7].

# 1 Introduction and main results

In recent years, models related to KPZ growth in a half-space were extensively studied. For example, [1] studied the ﬂuctuations of last passage percolation with exponential weights in a half-quadrant within the framework of Pfaﬃan Schur processes, [17] shows the ﬂuctuations of a one-dimensional polynuclear growth model in a half-space, the log-gamma polymer in a half space was also considered [13]. For a large amount of half-space models, the ﬂuctuations follow the GUE Tracy Widom distribution in the bulk. It is interesting to further investigate if these models converges to the Airy line ensemble.
