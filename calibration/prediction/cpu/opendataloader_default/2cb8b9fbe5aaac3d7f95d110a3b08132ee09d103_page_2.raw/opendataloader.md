2.2. Preparation of MRF. The preparation of MRF can be realized by existing technology [18, 19]. The detailed steps can be described as follows. Firstly, carrier liquid and surfactant aremixedwithcertainproportioninacontainer.Themixture is stirred evenly under water-bathing at 50∘C in a blender for 2 hours. Then, soft magnetic particles are poured slowly into the container while the blender is running. The particles are evenly distributed in the compound liquid after this step. Secondly, the mixture is milled in a planetary ball mill for about 10h. The aim of this step is to ensure that soft
