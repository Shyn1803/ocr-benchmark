# Further elective courses

Students must get 9 CFU/ECTS by choosing one or more courses offered by the University of Milan. The course of choice has to award a final mark. Please check https://mhr.cdl.unimi.it/en/courses/9-credit-courses. Moreover, students must get 3 CFU by choosing from internship, workshops, assessment of advanced computer skills, assessment of advanced knowledge foreign languages, traineeship abroad. Please check https://mhr.cdl.unimi.it/en/courses/3-credit-activities.
