3. Abstract Dynamic Programs

This section is a brief review of the dynamic programming framework from Sargent and Stachurski (2025). We repeat the key definitions to make the paper partly selfcontained.

- (ii) = {Tσ : σ ∈ Σ} is a nonempty family of order preserving self-maps on V .
