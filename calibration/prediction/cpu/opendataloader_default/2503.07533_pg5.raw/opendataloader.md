Remark 1. Convergence of the Markov process to the deterministic gradient flow describing trait dynamics occurs in the limit of small jumps σ2 → 0 [9]. In system (1), ε therefore acts as a small timescale separation parameter 0 < ε ≪ 1. On the slow timescale τ = εt we recover the canonical equation of adaptive dynamics in the limit ε → 0.
