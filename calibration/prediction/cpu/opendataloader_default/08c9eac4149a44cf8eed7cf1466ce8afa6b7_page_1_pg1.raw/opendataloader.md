# IMMEDIATE PAST PRESIDENT’S MESSAGE

![](<08c9eac4149a44cf8eed7cf1466ce8afa6b7_page_1_pg1_images/imageFile2.png>)

There have been a lot of changes in my life in the last two years, and I have decided to move from western Washington where I have lived all my life to Carlsbad, California. I have some family in the area and had met a friend there last year. She is a world traveler and an avid photographer. But the Northwest will always be my "home".

The travel bug is still with me having traveled to Europe, Africa, South America and Antarctica since we last met and with a few weeks in Southeast Asia scheduled for February.

Dear OPCC members,

I will send Jim my email address once I get settled so we can keep in contact. Thanks again for the friendships and I wish all of you a Great Christmas and all the best in the New Year.

My last five years as a member of OPCC have been very enjoyable and rewarding.

I have met many talented people and have made many friends, and have learned more about photography than I had hoped for.

Bob Mandich
