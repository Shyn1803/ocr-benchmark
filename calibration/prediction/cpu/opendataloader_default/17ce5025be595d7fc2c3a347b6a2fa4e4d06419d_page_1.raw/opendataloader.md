# Class Schedule - Fall 2004

## Computer Science

CS 173 Discrete Structures credit: 2 hours.

(C S 173) Studies discrete mathematical structures frequently encountered in the study of Computer Science. Topics will include sets, propositions, boolean algebra, induction, recursion, relations, functions, and graphs. Credit is not given for both CS 173 and MATH 213.
