## taking time out for taste

Memorable homemade sandwiches start with a visit to our Di Lusso Deli Company counter. This premium line of deli meats, cheeses, and condiments can be found in the deli case in select markets. Hormel pepperoni is another great choice for snacks that deliver taste and satisfaction. Our Di Lusso and other lines of pre-sliced meats generated double-digit sales gains in fiscal 2004.

## tempting mid-day meals

Casual lunchtime dining is a tasty mid-day booster with our Austin Blues BBQ and sandwiches made from Hormel Foods’ Bread Ready line of pre-sliced luncheon meats. With nearly half of the average family food dollar spent away from home, Hormel Foods has worked to become a preferred supplier of preportioned, pre-sliced, and pre-flavored items that build repeat Foodservice business. Foodservice sales were up 22 percent in fiscal 2004.
