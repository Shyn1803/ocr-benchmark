# Child Psychologist – Responsibilities/Duties

- • Study the mental and psychological development of children
- • Child counseling
- • Diagnosis and treatment of speech difficulty
- • Help a child overcome his/her limitations
- • Treat childhood learning disabilities
- • Research on child development issues
- • Counsel the whole family to help them adapt to the child with problem and help the child get well
