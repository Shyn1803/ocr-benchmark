- Example 3 (Roy Model) In the Roy model, individuals self-select into treatment when their beneﬁts from the treatment are greater than nonpecuniary costs for treatment participation. The extended Roy model assumes that the nonpecuniary cost is deterministic with the following selection equation:

D = 1{Y1 − Y0 ≥ µC (Z)},

where µC (Z) represents nonpecuniary costs with a vector of observables Z. Then treated (D = 1) and untreated people (D = 0) are the observed groups satisfying support restrictions {Y1 − Y0 ≥ µC (Z)} and {Y1 − Y0 < µC (Z)}, respectively.
