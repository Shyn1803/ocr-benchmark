The SPIE NCU student chapter will support the 7th Vacuum and Surface Sciences Conference of Asia and Australia. The 7th Vacuum and Surface Sciences Conference of Asia and Australia (VASSCAA-7) will be held from October 5th (Sunday) to 9th (Thursday), 2014 at National Tsing Hua University, Hsinchu, Taiwan.
